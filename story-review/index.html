Meet Rebekah Spencer

She turned her own questions into a place where others could ask theirs.

For more than 30 years, Rebekah navigated persistent personal health challenges. When familiar approaches left her wanting a deeper understanding, she began studying nutrition, natural wellness and the connections between everyday choices and whole-person well-being.

That education changed the direction of her life. Rebekah became a Holistic Health Practitioner, NES Practitioner and Reiki Master, while continuing to learn, teach and share what she discovered. Her goal was never to make wellness feel exclusive or intimidating. It was to make good information easier to reach.

She opened the first Rebekah’s Health & Nutrition in Lapeer in 2007: a small, welcoming store where people could slow down, compare options and speak with someone who would genuinely listen. Nearly two decades later, that same conversation-first philosophy guides every location.

“Take control of your health, and you take control of your life.”

A classroom, a marketplace, a meeting place

The stores were always meant to bring people together.

Rebekah’s regularly opens its doors to local practitioners, educators, makers and curious neighbors.

Health and wellness fairs introduce visitors to practitioners and hands-on demonstrations. Classes have covered subjects ranging from label reading and herbal traditions to sourdough baking and beekeeping. Seasonal markets create space for local food and artisan businesses. The common thread is practical education delivered face to face.

This community model gives beginners a comfortable place to ask questions and longtime wellness shoppers new ideas to explore. It also keeps more knowledge, relationships and dollars circulating close to home.

Rooted beyond the stores

Family, faith, nature and a life of continued learning.

Rebekah’s work is deeply connected to the life she values outside the business.

She and her husband, Mark, are building their dream home at Spencer’s Pond, where tending bees and spending time in nature offer a quieter rhythm. Mark is a beekeeper, bee-school instructor and Vice President of the Seven Ponds Bee Club; together, they share Spencer’s Honey and education about the gifts and importance of the hive.

Travel, family and the natural world continue to shape Rebekah’s curiosity. Those experiences return with her to the stores as new questions, fresh inspiration and the belief that wellness is not a finish line—it is a lifelong practice.
