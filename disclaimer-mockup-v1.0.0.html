Do not use information from this website to diagnose or treat a health problem or to prescribe, begin, stop or change any medication, supplement, diet, exercise program or other treatment.

Consult a qualified healthcare professional before making such changes, especially if you are pregnant or nursing, take prescription or over-the-counter medication, have a diagnosed condition, are preparing for surgery, or have or suspect you may have a health problem.
