What is LifeWave?

Small patches, explained without big promises.

LifeWave describes its products as non-transdermal wellness patches: they are worn on the skin, while the materials inside remain sealed.

According to LifeWave, the patches are designed to interact with infrared energy emitted by the body and reflect particular wavelengths toward specific points on the skin. That is the company's description of its technology—not a promise of a particular result.

Plain-language distinction: LifeWave does not describe these as patches that deliver a drug, supplement or ingredient through the skin. Product availability, directions and company claims may change, so use the current official listing and packaging as the source of truth.
