## Draft Shipping Policy copy

### Online-order fulfillment

Online orders are prepared at Rebekah’s Clarkston location. Please allow up to two business days for packing before shipment. Carrier transit time begins after USPS accepts the package and is separate from Rebekah’s handling time.

### Where Rebekah’s ships

At launch, online shipping is available only to eligible street addresses in the 48 contiguous United States and the District of Columbia. Rebekah’s does not ship online orders to PO boxes, Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, international destinations, or other excluded locations.

### Services, rates, and delivery estimates

Eligible orders may be offered USPS Ground Advantage or Priority Mail during checkout. Available services and charges are calculated using the delivery address, eligible products, cart weight, and the configured packing method. Rates and estimated transit times come from the carrier, may change, and are not guaranteed delivery dates.

### Product restrictions

Some products may be excluded from shipping because of carrier, safety, manufacturer, temperature, size, or destination restrictions. If an order contains an ineligible item, Rebekah’s may contact the customer to revise or cancel the affected item before fulfillment.

### Address accuracy

Customers are responsible for entering a complete deliverable street address. Rebekah’s is not responsible for delays or additional carrier charges caused by an incorrect or incomplete address, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

## Draft replacement for Terms & Conditions section 6

### 6. Purchases, payment, shipping, and returns

Rebekah’s may sell eligible products directly through the Website. By submitting an order, you offer to purchase the products shown in the order review at the displayed prices, taxes, and shipping charges. An automated acknowledgement does not guarantee acceptance. Rebekah’s may decline or cancel an order because of availability, pricing or description errors, suspected fraud, shipping restrictions, payment issues, or other lawful reasons. If payment was collected for a canceled order, the applicable amount will be returned through the original payment method.

Product availability, prices, promotions, and descriptions may change. Rebekah’s may correct an error before accepting or fulfilling an order. Backorders are not accepted unless Rebekah’s later states otherwise.

Online orders are limited to the shipping locations and delivery methods stated in the Shipping and Refund & Returns policies. Customers must provide accurate billing, contact, and delivery information. Returns, cancellations, damaged items, and refunds are governed by the Refund & Returns Policy and any additional terms displayed during checkout.

The Website also links to external shopping or referral providers, including Fullscript, Designs for Health, and other services. Purchases completed on an external provider’s website remain agreements with that provider and are governed by its prices, checkout, shipping, returns, privacy practices, and terms.

## Draft Privacy Policy ecommerce additions

### Orders and optional customer accounts

When a customer places an order or creates an optional account, the Website may collect contact details, billing and shipping addresses, order contents, account credentials, transaction status, customer notes, IP address, device or browser information, and related fraud-prevention or security data. Rebekah’s uses this information to process and fulfill orders, provide account and customer service, prevent misuse, keep required business records, and comply with legal obligations.

### Payments

Payment information will be handled by the payment processor selected before launch. Rebekah’s Website may receive transaction identifiers, authorization or failure status, card type, and limited card details such as the last four digits, but the final policy must reflect the processor’s actual integration and data practices. Do not publish a processor name until it is confirmed.

### Shipping and fulfillment

Rebekah’s may share the information needed to quote, purchase, track, and support shipment with USPS and any approved shipping technology provider. The final policy must identify the providers actually used at launch.

### Catalog, inventory, and order synchronization

WooCommerce will operate the online storefront. Revel and Kosmos may process product, inventory, customer, and order information only according to the final approved connection design. The final policy must describe the data that actually flows after the controlled integration tests are complete; this draft does not assume unverified writeback behavior.

### Retention and customer choices

Order and account information is retained for the time reasonably needed for fulfillment, customer service, accounting, tax, fraud prevention, warranty or return handling, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. A customer request to access, correct, export, or delete information may be limited where Rebekah’s must retain a record for one of those purposes.

## Draft Disclaimer ecommerce addition

Product descriptions, label images, ingredients, directions, warnings, availability, packaging, and manufacturer statements may change. Before using a product, review the current label and packaging supplied with it. Website information is not a substitute for those materials or for advice from an appropriate healthcare professional. Consult an appropriate healthcare professional regarding individual conditions, medications, pregnancy or nursing, allergies, sensitivities, or adverse reactions.

The responsibility and terms for a product purchased from an external partner remain with that provider. Rebekah’s onsite store policies apply only to products purchased directly through Rebekah’s Website.
