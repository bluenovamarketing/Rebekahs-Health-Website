What happens after design approval

The next steps are separate from this visual review

1. Client design approval

Confirm the appearance, wording, organization, and customer flow represented by these seven previews. The review labels and explanatory notes are not part of the future live pages.

2. Readiness and product setup

Confirm the remaining shipping, tax, account, inventory, policy, email, and payment information and provide the required Revel, Kosmos, USPS, and payment access. Blue Nova will select and prepare one representative product for an internal connection test.

3. Safe staging preparation

After every prerequisite and the work window are ready, create a restore point and protected staging clone, prevent indexing and email/payment side effects, reconcile legacy products, and reactivate only required commerce components.

4. Internal connection test

Blue Nova will validate Revel → Kosmos → WooCommerce with one product first. When the test passes, work continues directly to the remaining 24 products. Rebekah will be contacted only if a problem requires a business decision, additional information, or approval.

5. Complete pilot and storefront build

After the internal test passes, synchronize and reconcile the remaining 24 products without a separate approval gate; apply the approved storefront system; complete product content and store rules; then present the complete 25-product pilot storefront for client review and approval.

6. Acceptance, launch, and handoff

Pass integration, checkout, responsive, accessibility, SEO, analytics, performance, backup, email, refund, and fulfillment tests; obtain client approval, train staff, launch in a controlled window, and monitor stabilization.

Important: approving these mockups approves the visual direction. It does not approve unfinished product information or authorize launch. The connected store will still be tested on staging before anything goes live.
