Complete Phase Two plan

Current local work + gated future phases

1. Local design approval

Create, revise, and approve all seven responsive systems using controlled sample products. No WordPress or hosting changes occur during this phase.

2. Decisions, access, and pilot products

Approve shipping, tax, accounts, inventory rules, policies, and operational email; prepare the 25 Revel products; provide Revel, Kosmos, USPS, and payment access when required.

3. Safe staging preparation

After every prerequisite and the work window are ready, create a restore point and protected staging clone, prevent indexing and email/payment side effects, reconcile legacy products, and reactivate only required commerce components.

4. One-product integration proof

Connect Revel → Kosmos → WooCommerce and test one prepared simple or variation product first. Confirm eligibility, mapping, overwrite behavior, inventory, weight, categories, logs, and field ownership.

5. Twenty-five-product pilot and build

Synchronize and reconcile the remaining pilot products, apply the approved storefront system, complete product content, and configure the approved shipping, tax, account, email, inventory, and payment rules.

6. Acceptance, launch, and handoff

Pass integration, checkout, responsive, accessibility, SEO, analytics, performance, backup, email, refund, and fulfillment tests; obtain client approval, train staff, launch in a controlled window, and monitor stabilization.

Gate rule: no later phase starts just because the prior mockup exists. Every phase requires its listed approvals, access, data, tests, budget, and safe working window. Failed tests return to the responsible phase before launch.
