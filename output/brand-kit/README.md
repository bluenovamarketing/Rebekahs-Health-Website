# Rebekah's Health & Nutrition Brand Kit

## Contents

- `Rebekahs-Health-Nutrition-Brand-Guide.pdf` - complete client-facing guide
- `assets/logos/` - primary, stacked, icon, and reverse SVG directions plus the legacy logo
- `assets/previews/brand-board.png` - one-page visual quick reference
- `assets/social/` - Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube profile and campaign assets
  - Instagram avatar, five highlight covers, square post template, and Story template
  - TikTok avatar and vertical video-cover template
  - YouTube avatar, 2560x1440 channel banner, and 150px video watermark
- `tokens/` - CSS and JSON color, type, radius, spacing, and shadow tokens
- `social-profile-urls.md` - verified website, social, founder, and community profile links

## Important production note

Confirm the naming hierarchy (`Health & Nutrition` versus the legacy `Source` wording), secure client approval, and complete trademark/legal review before permanent signage, packaging, or large-scale production.
