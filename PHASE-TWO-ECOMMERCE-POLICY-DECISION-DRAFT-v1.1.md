## Use and limitation

This is a business-policy drafting aid, not legal advice and not approved website copy. It records confirmed operating decisions, identifies unresolved choices, and supplies draft language for review before public ecommerce launches.

## Confirmed operating facts

- Orders ship from Rebekah’s Clarkston location at 7093 Suite B, Dixie Highway, Clarkston, MI 48346.
- Allow two business days for packing before shipment.
- Shipping is limited to the continental United States.
- Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, and international shipping are excluded from launch.
- PO boxes are not accepted for shipping.
- Live USPS rates will be calculated from destination and cart weight during checkout.
- Backorders are disabled.
- Guest checkout is allowed and customer accounts are optional.
- Coupons, gift certificates, store credit, loyalty, and product reviews are excluded from the pilot.
- Product and order questions use 248-843-2011 unless Rebekah approves another channel.

## Draft Shipping Policy additions

> Online orders are prepared and shipped from Rebekah’s Clarkston location. Please allow up to two business days for packing before shipment. Delivery time begins after the carrier accepts the package and is separate from our handling time.

> At launch, shipping is available only to eligible street addresses within the continental United States. We do not ship to PO boxes, Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, or international destinations.

> Available USPS services and shipping charges are calculated during checkout using the delivery address, eligible products, cart weight, and configured packing method. Rates and estimated transit times are supplied by the carrier and may change.

> Some products may be excluded from shipping because of carrier, safety, manufacturer, temperature, size, or destination restrictions. If an order contains an ineligible item, Rebekah’s may contact the customer to revise or cancel the affected item before fulfillment.

## Draft Returns additions

Preserve the existing approved return window and condition requirements unless Rebekah changes them. Add the following concepts after the unresolved decisions above are answered:

> Contact Rebekah’s before mailing an online-order return so the team can confirm eligibility and provide the correct return instructions. Include the order number and proof of purchase. Products must meet the conditions and time limits stated in the Refund & Returns Policy.

> Original shipping charges are nonrefundable unless required by law or the return results from Rebekah’s error. Return-shipping responsibility depends on the reason for the return and will be confirmed before the item is mailed.

## Draft damaged, incorrect, lost, and delivered-package additions

> If an order arrives damaged or contains the wrong item, contact Rebekah’s within [CLIENT DECISION] days of delivery. Keep the product and packaging until the team confirms whether photographs, carrier documentation, or return shipment is needed.

> Carrier delays and missing-package claims are reviewed using the shipment tracking and carrier process. If tracking shows delivered but the package cannot be located, contact Rebekah’s within [CLIENT DECISION] days so the team can review the shipment and available carrier options.

## Draft cancellation addition

> Contact Rebekah’s as soon as possible to request cancellation. A cancellation is not guaranteed after an order enters packing, is transferred for fulfillment, or is accepted by the carrier. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method according to the payment provider’s processing time.
