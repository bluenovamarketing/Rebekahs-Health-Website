## Permanent version rule

- Each page, template, or reusable mockup system begins at `v1.1`.
- Each revision shown for review receives the next number: `v1.2`, `v1.3`, and so on. After `v1.9`, continue to `v1.10`.
- Existing numbered mockups continue from their highest recorded revision instead of restarting.
- Version sequences belong to each mockup independently.
- Earlier reviewed files remain preserved and are marked superseded; the review hub links to the current version only.
- Internal approval and client approval are separate gates tied to one exact version.
- Every new revision or approval change must update this register, the review hub, the Phase Two workbook, and `CLIENT-NOTES.md`.

## Status definitions

- **Draft:** being prepared locally and not yet shown for internal review.
- **Internal review:** ready for Todd's review.
- **Revision needed:** reviewed internally and requires a new numbered version.
- **Internally approved:** Todd approved this exact version for client review.
- **Awaiting client approval:** the exact internally approved version is with Rebekah or ready to be sent to her.
- **Client approved:** Rebekah approved this exact version; later changes require the next version and new approval.
- **Superseded:** preserved history replaced by a newer numbered version.

Local work only—no Cloudways, staging, live website, WordPress implementation, integrations, purchases, or paid services.
