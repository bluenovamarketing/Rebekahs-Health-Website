Meet Rebekah Spencer

For Rebekah, this has always been more than a store.

For more than 30 years, Rebekah searched for better ways to understand and support her own well-being. Frustrated by approaches that left her questions unanswered, she began studying nutrition, natural wellness and whole-person care.

That personal journey grew into a calling: help other people feel less overwhelmed, ask better questions and discover practical options that fit their lives. Today, Rebekah is a Holistic Health Practitioner, educator, health coach, author—and, most importantly, a mother to three incredible men.

“I wanted to create the kind of place I had been searching for—welcoming, knowledgeable and genuinely invested in people.”

The journey

From personal questions to a shared purpose.

01

The search for answers

Rebekah’s own wellness challenges inspired years of learning about nutrition, lifestyle choices and the body as a connected whole.

02

A place people could trust

In 2007, the first Rebekah’s opened with a simple promise: offer thoughtfully chosen products, useful education and patient, personal guidance.

03

A growing Michigan community

One neighborhood store became four—Lapeer, Clarkston, Grand Blanc and Lake Orion—each grounded in the same culture of listening and care.

04

Still learning, still sharing

Through in-store conversations, classes, events and the Wellness Source, the mission continues: make everyday wellness easier to understand and more empowering to pursue.

What guides us

Good wellness guidance starts with good values.

Our shelves matter—but how we serve people matters even more.

01

People before products

We listen first. Our role is to help you understand your options and make informed choices—not pressure you into buying more.

02

Education builds confidence

Clear, practical information can turn an overwhelming aisle into a manageable next step. Questions are always welcome here.

03

Local care has power
