Modern science + botanical knowledge

More than one way to know an ingredient.

The manufacturing laboratory combines traditional botanical evaluation with modern analytical technology.

Open a method to see what it may help evaluate. Available methods are selected according to the raw material or finished formula.

FTIRFourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy

Helps identify chemical compounds and confirm ingredient identity.

GCGas Chromatography

Evaluates chemical profiles and potency, and is commonly used in essential-oil analysis.

HP-TLCHigh Performance Thin Layer Chromatography

Helps identify botanical ingredients, including plant genus and species.

HPLCHigh Performance Liquid Chromatography

Isolates and measures specific chemical markers to evaluate certain plant compounds, vitamins and other ingredients.

ICP-MSPlasma Mass Spectrometry

Measures and quantifies heavy metals in applicable raw materials and finished products.

MicroMicroscopy & Macroscopy

Examines cellular structures and visible plant characteristics to help verify botanical identity.

MicrobioMicrobiological Testing

Evaluates applicable materials and products for microorganisms such as aerobic bacteria, yeast, mold, E. coli, Staphylococcus and Salmonella.

SenseOrganoleptic Evaluation

Uses traditional markers including taste, color, aroma and appearance as additional botanical quality evidence.

TitrateTitration

May be used to measure the potency of certain vitamins and minerals.

UV-VISUltraviolet-Visible Spectroscopy

Measures concentrations of specific compounds within certain botanical ingredients.

Why multiple methods matter: botanical identity, harvest, handling and extraction can influence a finished product. Combining techniques gives the laboratory several ways to evaluate ingredient identity and quality.

Heavy metals & pesticides

Quality is also about what should not be there.

Heavy-metal analysis is an important quality-control step for applicable raw materials and finished products.

ICP-MS technology may be used to quantify metals within an ingredient or formula. Pesticide testing is also performed when applicable based on the botanical, ingredient and established specifications.

ICP-MSMeasured safeguards

Advanced instrumentation can help quantify metals at very small concentrations, adding evidence to ingredient and finished-product review.
