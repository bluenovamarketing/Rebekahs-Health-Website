## Guardrails

- Avoid assigning the same primary intent to multiple pages without a documented reason.
- Do not create thin pages solely to target a keyword variation.
- Verify local and commercial relevance before recommending a new page.
- Record the source and date for keyword, Search Console, competitor, or other evidence.
