## Required checks before drafting

- Read the applicable canonical planning-sheet row and `Copy/Paste Prompt` for page-specific work.
- Confirm the search intent and avoid overlap with an existing page.
- Identify original, client-relevant information that makes the content useful.
- Define the desired user action and appropriate internal links.
- Separate draft approval from live publication approval.
