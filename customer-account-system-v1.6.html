Customer approval

Automatic, not manual

Customers create their own optional accounts with the normal email and password setup. Rebekah's staff will not approve accounts one by one.

Customer experience

No customer 2FA

Customers use the standard sign-in and password-reset flow. Guest checkout stays available and creates no account. Staff and administrator security is handled separately in the website backend.

Expected software cost

No separate account add-on planned

Guest checkout and the standard My Account features are included with WooCommerce core. No customer-verification extension or customer 2FA purchase is planned.

Client management

Low routine workload

Customers handle sign-in, password resets, and addresses themselves. Staff receive a written guide and video for occasional access or order questions and when to escalate a technical problem.
