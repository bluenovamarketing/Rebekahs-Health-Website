About the practitioner

Professional background

Dr. Kahin is a board-certified doctor of chiropractic and certified Integrative Functional Medicine practitioner. She is also certified in functional nutrition, dry needling, IASTM and physiotherapy, and holds chiropractic licenses in Georgia, Florida, Arizona and California.

She has served the Genesee County area since 2006. Her current directory description presents an approach centered on holistic functional medicine and helping patients pursue healthier lifestyles and wellness.
