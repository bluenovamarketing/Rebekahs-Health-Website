Who we are

This website is operated by Rebekah’s Health & Nutrition. Our website address is https://rebekahspureliving.com.

This policy describes information handled through this website. Information handled by a third-party website or service is also subject to that provider’s own privacy terms.

What personal information we collect and why

Contact forms and signups

If you send a message or submit a signup form, the site may collect the information shown in the form, such as your name, email address, phone number, preferred store and message. This information is used to respond to your request, route it to the appropriate team or provide the updates you requested.

Please do not submit sensitive health information through a general website form.

Comments

When visitors leave comments, the site collects the data shown in the comments form, along with the visitor’s IP address and browser user-agent string to help detect spam.

An anonymized string created from an email address may be provided to Gravatar to determine whether the visitor uses that service. After a comment is approved, the profile picture associated with Gravatar may be visible publicly with the comment. See Automattic’s privacy policy.

Media uploads

If visitors are permitted to upload images, they should avoid uploading images with embedded location data (EXIF GPS). Other visitors may be able to download and extract location data from publicly available images.

Cookies

Cookies are small files stored by a browser. The site may use cookies needed for core website functions and may use additional cookies only as configured for the live site.

Comments

If you leave a comment, you may opt in to saving your name, email address and website in cookies so you do not need to enter those details again. These convenience cookies last for one year.

Login and publishing

If you visit the login page, a temporary cookie is set to determine whether the browser accepts cookies. It contains no personal data and is discarded when the browser closes.

When an authorized user logs in, cookies save login information and display choices. Login cookies last for two days, screen-option cookies last for one year and “Remember Me” login cookies persist for two weeks. Logging out removes the login cookies. If an authorized user edits or publishes an article, an additional cookie containing the post ID is saved for one day.

Embedded content from other websites

Articles and pages may include embedded videos, images, social posts or other content. Embedded content behaves in the same way as if the visitor went directly to the provider’s website.

Those providers may collect information, use cookies, add third-party tracking and monitor interaction with the embedded content, including when a visitor has an account with that provider and is logged in.

Who we share information with

Visitor comments may be checked through an automated spam-detection service. Information submitted through website forms may also be handled by the service providers that deliver, secure or store those submissions on Rebekah’s behalf.

Rebekah’s should identify each active provider and the purpose for which information is shared before this draft is published.

How long we retain information

If you leave a comment, the comment and its metadata are retained indefinitely so the site can recognize and approve follow-up comments automatically instead of holding them in a moderation queue.

For users who register on the website, if any, the site stores the personal information provided in their user profile. Users can see, edit or delete their personal information at any time, except they cannot change their username. Website administrators can also see and edit that information.

Your choices and rights

If you have an account on this site or have left comments, you can request an exported file of the personal information held about you, including information you provided. You can also request that personal information be erased. This does not include information Rebekah’s must keep for administrative, legal or security purposes.

Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply. Requests may need to be verified before they are completed.

How we protect information

Rebekah’s should describe the administrative, technical and organizational safeguards that are actually used to protect website information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The final policy should also document verified procedures for handling a data incident and whether automated decision-making or profiling is used.
