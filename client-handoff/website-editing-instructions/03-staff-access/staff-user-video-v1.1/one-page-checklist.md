# Add, Change, or Remove a Staff WordPress User

**Version:** v1.1  
**Required role:** Administrator only

## Before you begin

- Confirm the staff member's full name and unique work email.
- Confirm whether the request is to add, change, reset, or remove access.
- Confirm the minimum approved role. Editor is sufficient for routine practitioner and event work.
- Never share or reuse another person's login.

## Add a staff user

1. Sign in with your own Administrator account.
2. Open **Users > Add New User**.
3. Enter a permanent username and the person's unique work email.
4. Enter the person's first and last name.
5. Keep the secure generated password and user-notification option.
6. Choose **Editor** for routine approved content work. Use Administrator only when specifically approved.
7. Review every field, select **Add New User** once, and wait for confirmation.
8. Have the staff member use the invitation, set a private password, and verify the needed menus.

## Change or reset access

1. Open **Users > All Users** and match both name and email.
2. Use **Edit** to change only the approved Role, then save once.
3. Use **Send password reset** instead of requesting or sharing passwords.

## Remove access

1. Verify the exact departing account and select **Delete**.
2. Choose **Attribute all content to** an approved continuing Administrator, such as Rebekah.
3. Never choose **Delete all content** without Blue Nova approval.
4. Confirm the old account can no longer sign in.

**Always finish with: Confirm the person > assign the minimum role > verify the resulting access. Stop and contact Blue Nova when a role, Administrator account, or content reassignment is uncertain.**
