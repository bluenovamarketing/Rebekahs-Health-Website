## Scene 1 — Add, change, or remove a staff WordPress user

This lesson shows an authorized Administrator how to add, change, reset, or remove a staff WordPress account safely. Every person must have a separate login. We will use the minimum role the person needs, protect passwords, and remove access without accidentally deleting website content.

## Scene 2 — Required WordPress access

You must already have the Administrator role to manage users. Editor is enough for routine practitioner and event work, but an Editor cannot safely manage accounts. Never give Administrator access simply because someone cannot see a menu. If the correct access is uncertain, stop and ask Blue Nova.

## Scene 3 — Gather the approved account information first

Before opening Users, gather the staff member's full name, unique work email address, and approved WordPress role. Confirm whether you are adding a person, changing an existing account, resetting access, or removing access. Never reuse another person's username, email, or password.

## Scene 4 — Open Users and choose the correct action

Sign in at rebekahspureliving.com forward slash W P dash admin using your own Administrator account. In the left menu, select Users. Choose Add New User to add someone. Choose All Users to change, reset, or remove an existing account. Stop if Users is missing.

## Scene 5 — Create a permanent username and unique email

For a new user, enter a simple username the staff member will recognize. Then enter that person's unique work email address. Check both entries carefully. A WordPress username cannot be changed later, and a shared email or shared login makes it impossible to know who made a change.

## Scene 6 — Complete the name and invitation settings

Enter the staff member's real first and last name. Leave the secure generated password in place. Keep the option selected that sends the new user an email about the account. WordPress will give the person a private setup link. Do not create an easy password or send a password yourself.

## Scene 7 — Choose the minimum role the person needs

Choose Editor for approved practitioner and event work. Editor is the normal routine-content role. Choose Administrator only for an owner or trusted website manager who must manage users, plugins, or settings. If the person's duties do not clearly match a role, stop and ask Blue Nova before creating the account.

## Scene 8 — Add the user once and wait for confirmation

Review the username, email, name, notification option, and role. Select Add New User one time, then wait for WordPress to confirm success. If WordPress says the username or email already exists, do not create a variation. Return to All Users and find the existing account.

## Scene 9 — Have the staff member complete the first login

The staff member opens the WordPress invitation email, creates a strong private password, and signs in at rebekahspureliving.com forward slash W P dash admin. Ask the person to confirm that the required menu is visible and that unrelated Administrator controls are not available. Never ask them to share their password.

## Scene 10 — Change an existing role carefully

To change access, open Users, then All Users. Match both the staff name and email before selecting Edit. Change only the Role field to the newly approved role and save once. Ask the person to sign out and sign back in before checking the new access.

## Scene 11 — Use password reset instead of sharing passwords

If a staff member cannot sign in, use Send password reset from All Users, or direct the person to Lost your password on the login screen. The staff member follows the private email and chooses a new password. Never ask for, store, text, or email another person's password.

## Scene 12 — Remove access without deleting website content

For a departing staff member, verify the exact account and select Delete. When WordPress asks about the person's content, choose Attribute all content to an approved continuing Administrator, such as Rebekah. Never choose Delete all content unless Blue Nova has reviewed and approved it. Confirm the old account can no longer sign in.

## Scene 13 — Finish by confirming the correct access

Finish every user change by confirming the exact person, the minimum approved role, and the resulting access. Each staff member keeps a separate login. Remove access promptly when employment or duties change. If an Administrator account, role, password, or content reassignment is uncertain, stop and contact Blue Nova.
