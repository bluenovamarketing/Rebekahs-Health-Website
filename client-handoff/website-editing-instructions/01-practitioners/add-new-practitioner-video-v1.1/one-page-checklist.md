# Add a New Practitioner or Practice Listing

Required access: WordPress Editor or Administrator. Editor is enough.

1. Go to `https://rebekahspureliving.com/wp-admin` and sign in with your own account.
2. Select **Medical Practicioners → All Medical Practicioners**.
3. Select **Add New Medical Practitioner** once.
4. Keep the new record as a **Draft** until every field is complete.
5. Open **Medical Practicioner fields** under Meta Boxes.
6. Choose only the approved **Medical Service** category or categories.
7. Enter the approved **Medical Center** and exact **Medical Practitioner Full Name**.
8. Add the approved description in **Visual** mode and preserve paragraph breaks.
9. Add only approved public contact details. Test the phone number, email, and website.
10. Review every field against the approved information.
11. Select **Publish** or **Save** once and wait for confirmation.
12. Return to the record list and clear only the applicable record, Breeze, or Varnish cache.
13. Open `https://rebekahspureliving.com/practitioners/` with no extra address text.
14. Search the name, practice, or city; check the category and every public detail.
15. Open **View full listing** and repeat the check on a phone-size screen.

Do not create unnecessary duplicate records for one practice or team. There is no separate public practitioner page to manage.

Always finish: **SAVE → CLEAR APPLICABLE CACHE → VERIFY LIVE**
