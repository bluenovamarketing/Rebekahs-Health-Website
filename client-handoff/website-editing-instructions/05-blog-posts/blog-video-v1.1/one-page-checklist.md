# Create or Update a Blog Post Safely

**Version:** v1.1  
**Required role:** Editor or Administrator; Editor is sufficient

## Prepare first

- Final approved title and article copy
- Approved existing category
- Approved featured image and useful alt text
- Exact internal and external links
- Approved search title and description

## Create or update

1. Open **Posts**. Choose **Add New Post** or match the exact existing title and select **Edit**.
2. Keep new work in **Draft**.
3. Enter the approved title and complete article in the Visual editor.
4. Use clear headings, short paragraphs, and useful lists.
5. Choose the correct existing category. Do not create or guess categories.
6. Add the approved featured image and descriptive alt text.
7. Link descriptive words and test every changed link.
8. Complete the approved SEOPress title and meta description without keyword stuffing.
9. Preview the full article at desktop and phone sizes.
10. Use **Publish** only for a complete approved new article; use **Update** for an approved existing article change.
11. Click once and wait for WordPress confirmation.

## Verify publicly

1. Clear only the applicable cache.
2. Open the exact public article URL with no extra address text.
3. Verify the visible copy, image, category, author, and links.
4. Check the Blog archive and repeat at phone size.

**Always finish with: Draft > preview > publish or update once > clear applicable cache > verify the article and Blog archive on desktop and phone.**
