# Website Editing Safety and Recovery

**Version:** v1.1  
**Required access:** Use the minimum assigned role; Editor is sufficient for routine approved content

## Safe start

1. Confirm the live domain: `rebekahspureliving.com/wp-admin`.
2. Use only your own named login.
3. Confirm the exact page, post, event, practitioner, or other record.
4. Gather the final approved replacement information before editing.
5. Stop if the task, record, role, or wording is uncertain.

## Safe edit

1. Change only the fields named in the approved request.
2. Leave unrelated copy, images, categories, links, dates, and settings unchanged.
3. Keep new or uncertain work in **Draft** and use **Preview**.
4. Check images, links, names, dates, times, prices, addresses, phone numbers, store details, and health wording.
5. Do not change Appearance, Plugins, Settings, users, Cloudways, servers, payments, store integrations, or optimization controls without separate authorization.

## Save and verify

1. Select the correct **Save**, **Update**, or **Publish** action once.
2. Wait for WordPress confirmation.
3. Clear only the applicable page or record cache; use broader purges only when approved.
4. Open the exact canonical public URL with no extra address text.
5. Verify the visible result and affected controls at desktop and phone sizes.

## Stop and contact Blue Nova when

- The screen or menu does not match the instructions.
- The correct record or approved information is uncertain.
- WordPress reports an error or does not confirm the save.
- The live result is wrong, missing, broken, or still stale.

**Always finish with: Correct item > approved change only > save once > clear applicable cache > verify publicly.**
