WEBSITE EDITING INSTRUCTIONS
Rebekah's Health & Nutrition

WATCH A VIDEO IN GOOGLE DRIVE

1. Open the Website Editing Instructions folder.
2. Open the folder for the task you want to complete.
3. Double-click the video file.
4. Select the Play button.
5. Use the full-screen button if the WordPress screen is difficult to read.

DOWNLOAD A COPY TO YOUR COMPUTER

1. Find the video in Google Drive.
2. Right-click the video, or select the three-dot More Actions menu.
3. Select Download.
4. If Google displays a download warning for a large video, confirm that you want to download the file.
5. Save the file in a folder on your computer that you will remember.
6. Open the downloaded MP4 file to confirm that it plays before relying on the saved copy.

You may download and keep copies of these training videos for your business records, including if Blue Nova Marketing no longer manages the website.

SAFETY REMINDER

Follow the exact video for the task you are completing. If your WordPress screen does not match the video, stop before saving and contact Blue Nova Marketing.
